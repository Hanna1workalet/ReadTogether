CHAPTER EIGHTEEN

A Different Life

She had thought the purpose was to find the right life. As though somewhere, hidden among the shelves, there was a perfect version waiting to forgive every wrong turn.

But there was no perfect life. There were only lives: complicated, surprising, unfinished. Each one asked something of her.

She placed a hand against the nearest shelf and felt, for the first time, not the weight of everything she had missed, but the lightness of everything still ahead.
